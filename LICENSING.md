# Licensing

FPVLink is source-available, not open source. Most people need nothing from this
page: if you are building one for yourself and flying for fun, you are covered
and you owe nothing.

| You are | What you need |
|---|---|
| Building one for yourself, flying for fun, learning, tinkering | **Nothing.** Go build it |
| A club, school, charity or public body using it | **Nothing.** Explicitly permitted |
| Doing something commercial with it | Ask first — see below |

The licence is the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

---

## What counts as commercial

Commercial rights are reserved. In practice that means these need permission:

- Selling FPVLink, or selling hardware with FPVLink on it
- Running paid, ticketed or sponsored events with it
- Building a commercial product or paid service on top of it
- Paid client video work produced with it

And these do not — they are ordinary noncommercial use and need nothing:

- Building a box for yourself and flying it
- Club nights, practice sessions, informal meets, anything with no gate fee
- Charity, school and public-body use, regardless of how it is funded
- Filming your own flying, including putting it on YouTube

## If you want to do something commercial

**Open an issue and ask.** There is no price list and nothing is for sale right
now — this project is not set up to take payments or to support commercial
users, and pretending otherwise would waste your time.

That is not a brush-off. Permission may well be granted, case by case, and
asking costs nothing. Reserving the rights now simply keeps the option open
rather than giving it away by default, which is a decision that cannot be
reversed once made.

If a commercial licence is offered later, it will be documented here.

## Supporting the project

If FPVLink is useful to you and you want to support the work, GitHub Sponsors is
the way. It is a straightforward donation: no tier buys support, priority, or
influence over the roadmap, and nothing here is contingent on it.
